UPI Hub privacy policy

UPI Hub (techswitch.upihub) is a QR scanner that hands UPI payment requests to a UPI app you choose. This policy explains what the app does with data.

Summary

UPI Hub does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and stores nothing on a server. The app has no internet permission, no account, no analytics and no advertising. Everything happens on your phone.

What the app uses, and why

DataUseLeaves your phone?
CameraLive preview to read QR codes. Frames are processed in memory and never saved.No
Images you pick or shareRead once to find a QR code, then discarded.No
QR code and payment details (UPI ID, payee name, amount, note)Shown to you, then passed to the UPI app you select so it can complete the payment.Only to that app, when you tap pay
List of installed UPI appsShow you which apps can pay.No
Settings (default app, app order, hidden apps)Stored privately on your phone.No

UPI Hub never sees your UPI PIN, bank account or card details. The UPI app you choose handles the payment under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

Camera, to scan QR codes. You can deny it and still pay from a screenshot or by typing a UPI ID.

Children

UPI Hub is not directed at children under 18.

Deleting your data

All data stays on your phone. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it deletes everything.
