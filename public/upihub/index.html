install in a minute

Both files come straight from the UPI Hub release on GitHub, built there: the APK signed, the IPA unsigned until there is an App Store build.

  1. download the apk

    Tap download apk on your Android phone. Chrome may warn that the file can harm your device; that warning appears for every app installed from outside the Play Store.

  2. allow the install

    Open the file. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser or Files app; turn it on, go back and tap install.

  3. allow the camera

    On first launch, allow the camera and pick your default UPI app from the ones on your phone.

  4. scan and pay

    Point at a UPI QR, confirm the app, and finish the payment with your PIN in that app.

  5. on an iphone

    Download the IPA on a Mac or PC, then install it with AltStore or Sideloadly signed in with your Apple ID. On the iPhone, trust your Apple ID under Settings, General, VPN & Device Management, then open UPI Hub. Sign it again every 7 days with a free Apple ID.

private by design

UPI Hub has no account, no ads, no tracking and no internet permission. It never sees your PIN or bank details.

  2. check the file

  3. updates

    Later versions are signed with the same key and install over this one. New releases appear on the GitHub releases page.
